About Your Programme

Level 3 Certificate in Assessing Vocational Achievement

Welcome to the Level 3 Certificate in Assessing Vocational Achievement (QRN: 601/6104/8). This nationally recognised qualification is designed for professional assessors working in vocational education and training.

This new integrated model combines traditional assessment methods with live tracking, giving you real-time visibility of your progress whilst maintaining rigorous quality assurance standards.

Programme Structure

Your qualification is structured across three units:

Unit 1: Understanding the Principles and Practices of Assessment covers the foundations of assessment practice, including principles, methods, planning, decision-making, quality assurance, information management, and legal requirements.

Unit 2: Assess Occupational Competence in the Work Environment focuses on assessing learner competence in real work settings, including planning, decision-making, feedback, and review processes.

Unit 3: Assess Vocational Skills, Knowledge and Understanding extends assessment practice to vocational skills development, covering similar learning outcomes to Unit 2 but applied to skills and knowledge contexts.

The New Integrated Assessment Model

Unlike traditional programmes, this qualification uses a live integrated model where every assessment decision you make is tracked in real time. When your assessor marks your work and records an outcome (Pass, Refer, or Pending), that decision automatically updates your Assessment Plan, creating a complete audit trail of your learning journey.

This approach provides several benefits:

You see exactly where you are in your learning at any moment. Your assessor can track patterns across assessments. Quality assurance is built in through the audit trail. You have a comprehensive record of evidence for final verification.

How Assessments Are Recorded

Each learning outcome has a planned end date that you set at the start. When you submit work for assessment, your assessor will evaluate it against the learning outcome criteria and record one of three outcomes:

Pass: You have met all criteria for this learning outcome.

Refer: You need to resubmit after addressing feedback.

Pending: Assessment is in progress or awaiting additional evidence.

Each attempt is logged in your Assessment Plan with the date, assessor name, and outcome. This creates a complete record of your assessment journey.

Your Assessment Plan

Your Assessment Plan document is your personal learning record. It tracks:

All three units and their learning outcomes. Your planned completion dates for each learning outcome. Every assessment attempt you make, including date and assessor name. The outcome of each assessment (Pass, Refer, or Pending). Observation records where applicable. Your sign-off and formal record of completion.

Visit the Assessment Plan tab to review and update your planned dates and track your progress.

Assessment Documents & Resources

Access assignment briefs, worksheets, and supporting materials in the Assessment Documents tab. Each unit has its own set of resources to guide your learning and assessment.

Getting Started

Start by visiting the Assessment Plan tab and filling in your planned end dates for each learning outcome. Think realistically about when you registered and when you'd like to complete each unit. Your assessor will use these dates to help track your progress and provide support where needed.

Your personal learning plan and ongoing assessment record

About Your Assessment Plan

This Assessment Plan is your personal learning record for the Level 3 Certificate in Assessing Vocational Achievement. It tracks your progress across all three units and shows every assessment attempt you make.

Your assessor will use this document to record when they have assessed your work, the date they assessed it, and whether you have passed or need to resubmit (referred). This creates a complete audit trail of your learning journey.

Before you start any work: Please complete the "Planned End Date" field for each learning outcome below. Think about when you registered on the programme and plan realistically when you would like to complete each unit and learning outcome. Your assessor will use these dates to help track your progress.
